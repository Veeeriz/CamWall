CamWall shows your cameras on Apple TV: a live camera wall, fullscreen cameras, a timeline of Frigate's events, clip playback and PTZ control.

It talks directly to your own [Frigate NVR](https://frigate.video) server and cameras. There is no CamWall account, no cloud service and no tracking.

## Features

- Camera wall with several layouts, fullscreen in place, Monitor Mode and Birdseye
- Timeline of Frigate reviews and recordings: play, save clips, mark reviewed
- Live detection banner with Watch Live and Play Clip
- PTZ control with presets, through Frigate or directly over ONVIF
- Main and substreams, health badges, and tiles that never go black
- Demo mode to try the app without a camera system

## Requirements

- Apple TV with tvOS 17 or later
- A Frigate server on your network, or cameras with an RTSP stream
