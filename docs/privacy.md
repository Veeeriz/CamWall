_Effective 19 October 2026_

**CamWall does not collect, store or share any personal data.** The developer of CamWall receives nothing from the app.

## What stays on your Apple TV

- **Settings** (server addresses, camera list, layouts, preferences) are stored locally on the device.
- **Passwords and tokens** for Frigate, cameras and MQTT are stored in the device's keychain.
- **Certificates** of servers you trust are remembered on the device.

Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Who the app talks to

CamWall connects only to the servers and cameras **you** add: your Frigate server, your cameras (RTSP, ONVIF) and, if you set one up, your MQTT broker. Video, snapshots, events and credentials go directly between your Apple TV and those devices. They never pass through any server run by the developer.

CamWall contains:

- no analytics or tracking,
- no advertising,
- no third-party SDKs that collect data,
- no account or sign-up.

## Purchases

Trials and purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. See [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your Apple TV settings, Apple may provide anonymised crash reports. You can turn this off in your Apple TV's privacy settings.

## Contacting support

If you email <camwall@veerman.se> or open a GitHub issue, the details you choose to send (such as your email address) are used only to answer you and are not shared. You can ask for an email conversation to be deleted at any time.

## Children

CamWall does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
