# FAQ

The most common questions are answered on the [support page](https://veeriz.github.io/CamWall/support/).

**Does CamWall need an account or cloud service?**
No. It talks only to your own Frigate server and cameras.

**Does it work outside my home network?**
Only if your Apple TV can reach Frigate, for example over a VPN.

**Which cameras are supported?**
Anything Frigate can restream, plus cameras with a plain RTSP stream. PTZ works through Frigate or directly over ONVIF.

**How many cameras can I show at once?**
It depends on the Apple TV model. CamWall caps autoplaying streams per model; substreams count as half. The cap can be turned off.

**Can it show alerts while I watch other apps?**
No — tvOS doesn't allow apps to show alerts over other apps or run in the background.
